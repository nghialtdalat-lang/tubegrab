Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

TubeGrab is a personal desktop application used only by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner chooses to the owner's own YouTube channel, with title, description, tags, thumbnail and a scheduled publish time.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to show the channel name and picture, and to read the channel's own uploaded videos and their scheduled publish times so uploads are not scheduled at the same time.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth token and the upload schedule are stored only in local files on the owner's computer.
  • No Google user data is sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. The app has no backend server, no analytics and no advertising.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than the features described above.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app folder deletes all locally stored data.

TubeGrab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
